What is Direct Debit?

Direct Debit is a secure payment method that allows approved recurring payments to be collected automatically from a user’s bank account. It can be used for bills, school fees, subscriptions, insurance, loans and business payments.

Why Direct Debit Pakistan?

Less Missed Payments

Users can avoid late fees by scheduling recurring payments automatically.

Better Cash Flow

Businesses can collect payments on time with clear customer authorization.

User Control

Customers approve, view and manage payment mandates before collections happen.

How It Works

01

Verify Identity

User signs in through Pak ID or verifies using OTP.

02

Authorize Mandate

The user approves a payment instruction for a specific merchant.

03

Pay Automatically

Payments are collected securely on the agreed date.

FAQs

Can users cancel a Direct Debit?

Yes. Users should be able to view, manage or cancel active payment mandates.

Is Direct Debit only for businesses?

No. It benefits individuals, businesses, banks, fintechs and service providers.

What can Direct Debit be used for?

It can be used for bills, fees, subscriptions, insurance, rent and other recurring payments.
